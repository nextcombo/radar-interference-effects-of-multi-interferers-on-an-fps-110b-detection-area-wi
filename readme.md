# Radar Interference: Effects of Multi-Interferers on AN/FPS-110B Detection Area [with MATLAB Code]

\n        \n  
Exposure area calculation
\n        \n  
\n        \n  
1. Taking the jammer = jamming AN/FPS-110B radar as an example, the exposed area of the radar in the absence of interference (red dashed line).
2. The exposed area of the radar under a single high-power interference scenario, assuming that all jammers are aligned with the main lobe of the radar (red solid line).
3. Exposure zones of radar in multiple power interference situations (green solid line).
4. The radar's exposed area for 5 jammers at fixed (-π/6, -π/12, 0, π/12, π/6) equilateral angles. (Yellow solid line).
5. The radar's exposure zone for 100 jammers at fixed (-π/6, π/6) equilateral angles. (Black solid line).
The exposed area of a single high-power interference machine accounts for 0.872739%.
The exposed area of 5 jammers accounts for 0.700340%.
The exposed area of 100 jammers is 105261567895.109950.
The exposed area of 100 jammers accounts for 0.544719%.
The exposed area of 200 jammers is 93577409786.316284 square meters.
The exposed area of 200 jammers accounts for 0.484255%.
